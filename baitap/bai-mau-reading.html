Sample Reading – Community Gardens

Đây là bài mẫu. Thầy/cô có thể xoá file này sau khi đưa bài thật lên.

In many cities, empty plots of land are being turned into community gardens. Local residents share the space, grow vegetables and flowers, and take turns watering the plants. Supporters say these gardens make neighbourhoods greener and give people a reason to meet each other.

However, community gardens also face problems. Some groups lose their land when the owner decides to sell it, and others struggle to find enough volunteers during the summer holidays. Even so, most gardeners believe the benefits are worth the effort.

1. What is the main idea of the passage?

2. According to the passage, who looks after the plants?

3. Which problem is mentioned in the passage?
